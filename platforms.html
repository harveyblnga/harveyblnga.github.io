Core Infrastructure

Reusable systems that help organisations deliver digital services, manage data, process payments, and communicate with users more reliably.

These infrastructure components power Belenga Dev platforms and can also support partner and client systems.

Infrastructure Layers

Payment Engine

Supports payments, subscriptions, account credits, and billing processes across digital services.

It helps organisations manage transactions reliably, whether they are handling customer payments, service access, or credit-based workflows.

Powers: MeterWatch, ShareholderView

Fulfillment Engine

Manages the automated delivery of digital services, reports, and background processing tasks.

It helps ensure that important actions are completed consistently, even when processes depend on multiple steps or external services.

Powers: MeterWatch, DeepDiveData

Explore

Data Intelligence Layer

Organises complex datasets into structured systems that support analysis, dashboards, and decision-making.

It helps transform fragmented information into reliable insights that can power data platforms, reporting tools, and operational visibility.

Powers: ShareholderView, DeepDiveData, MeterWatch

Explore

Messaging Infrastructure

Enables communication through WhatsApp, messaging APIs, and media-based workflows.

It supports automated messaging, user interaction, and information collection through accessible digital channels.

Powers: VoiceBridge, MeterWatch

Explore

Automation & Orchestration

Connects different tools and processes so that digital services can run smoothly across systems.

It helps reduce manual work, improve reliability, and support service delivery in complex or low-connectivity environments.

Powers: MeterWatch, VoiceBridge, DeepDiveData
