Our Platforms

ShareholderView

Financial intelligence for Zimbabwe's capital markets.

What it does: Provides investors, analysts, and researchers with structured insights on Zimbabwean listed companies, including shareholder data, dividends, and company performance.

How it works: Collects and organizes financial and market data, transforming it into clear dashboards and analytics workflows.

Status: Live platform

Live product

DeepDiveData

An interactive analytics platform that helps organisations explore and understand large public and private datasets.

What it does: Helps organisations explore large public and private datasets through interactive analytics and visualizations.

How it works: Combines structured data storage with flexible analysis features that allow users to explore trends and relationships across datasets.

Status: Live platform

Live product

MeterWatch

Energy intelligence for prepaid electricity users.

What it does: Helps households and organisations track electricity usage, understand tariff bands, and manage prepaid token purchases.

How it works: Uses electricity consumption data and predictive models to estimate usage patterns and provide alerts and insights.

Status: Live platform

Live product

VoiceBridge

An AI platform for collecting voice data in African languages.

What it does: Provides infrastructure for collecting and managing speech datasets used to train AI systems.

How it works: Supports distributed voice data collection through mobile, web, and messaging platforms, with workflows for annotation and quality review.

Status: Related project
